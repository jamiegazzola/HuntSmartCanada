# HuntSmart Canada

HuntSmart Canada is a data-backed hunting research platform for British Columbia and Alberta hunters.

**Website:** https://huntsmartcanada.ca/

## British Columbia research

- [BC Hunting Research Guide](https://huntsmartcanada.ca/bc-hunting/)
- [BC Hunting Research Questions](https://huntsmartcanada.ca/bc-hunting/questions/)
- [BC LEH Historical Data Explorer](https://huntsmartcanada.ca/bc-hunting/leh-data-explorer/)
- [How Does BC LEH Work?](https://huntsmartcanada.ca/blog/how-does-bc-leh-work/)
- [BC General Open Season Guide](https://huntsmartcanada.ca/bc-hunting/general-open-season/)
- [Data Sources & Methodology](https://huntsmartcanada.ca/methodology/)
- [Research & Citation Links](https://huntsmartcanada.ca/research/)

HuntSmart organizes public hunting information, historical draw context, maps and research tools. Current legal requirements should always be verified against official provincial publications.
